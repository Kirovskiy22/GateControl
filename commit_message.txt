fix(esp32): update gate sensor pin to IO35 in v1.4.0 firmware

Moved the gate sensor away from the IO12 strapping pin, which caused
hardware lockups on boot. Pin IO35 is now configured. As IO35 is an
input-only pin lacking an internal pull-up resistor, the firmware
was adjusted from INPUT_PULLUP to INPUT, and comments added specifying
the requirement of an external 10k resistor.
